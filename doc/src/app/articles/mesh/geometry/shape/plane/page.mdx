# [TgdGeometryPlane](docs/classes/TgdGeometryPlane.html)

<Demo />

A rectangle made of two triangles (drawn as a `TRIANGLE_STRIP`), centered on the origin.

- `sizeX`, `sizeY`: dimensions of the rectangle (default to `1`).
- `vecX`, `vecY`: the axes of the plane (default to `[1, 0, 0]` and `[0, 1, 0]`).
  The normal is `vecX × vecY`, and the front face looks in its direction.
- `uv0`, `uv1`: texture coordinates of the bottom-left and top-right corners
  (default to `[0, 0]` and `[1, 1]`). Use them to show only a part of a texture.

In this demo, the left plane shows the whole texture, while the right one is
tilted and only shows its upper right quarter.
