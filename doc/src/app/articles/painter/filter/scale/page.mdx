## [TgdFilterScale](docs/classes/TgdFilterScale.html)

<Demo />

Fits the texture into the output while keeping its aspect ratio, then scales it around the center.

- `mode: "cover"`: the texture fills the whole output, and the overflowing parts are cropped.
- `mode: "contain"`: the whole texture is visible, and the empty parts are transparent.

`scale` is applied after the fit, and values greater than 1 enlarge the texture.
It can be a single number (same scale on X and Y), a `TgdVec2` or an `[x, y]` array.

```ts
const scaleFilter = new TgdFilterScale({ mode: "contain", scale: [1, 0.5] })
context.add(new TgdPainterFilter(context, { texture, filters: [scaleFilter] }))
```

`mode` and `scale` are public fields, so you can change them at any time.
