# [TgdPainterFilter](docs/classes/TgdPainterFilter.html)

Filtering is the process of transforming a texture.

You can use existing filters:

- [Anti-aliasing](#anti-aliasing): FXAA, to smooth the edges of framebuffers and deferred shading.
- [Blur](#blur): blur along one direction (use a pair of them for a bidirectional blur).
- [Chromatic Aberration](#chromatic-aberration)
- [Deferred shading](#/articles/painter/deferred-shading): lighting, shadows, cavity and ambient occlusion from G-buffer textures.
- [Glow](#glow): bloom around the emissive parts, with Dual Filtering.
- [Hue Rotation](#hue)
- [Multiply](#multiply): multiply every pixel by a color.
- [Scale](#scale): fit the texture to the screen (`cover` or `contain`), then scale it.
- [Verbatim](#verbatim): paint the texture as it is.
- [Zoom](#zoom): zoom and translate the texture.

Even combine several of them together:

- [Several filters](#several)
- [Bloom effect](#bloom)



Or write [your own](#custom).
