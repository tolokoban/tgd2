## [TgdFilterZoom](docs/classes/TgdFilterZoom.html)

<Demo />

Zooms into the texture around its center, then moves it.

- `zoom`: values greater than 1 enlarge the texture, and values lower than 1 shrink it.
- `translation`: a `TgdVec2` in texture coordinates (`[1, 0]` moves by the full width of the texture).

```ts
const zoomFilter = new TgdFilterZoom({ zoom: 2 })
zoomFilter.translation.reset(0.1, 0)
context.add(new TgdPainterFilter(context, { texture, filters: [zoomFilter] }))
```

What appears outside of the texture depends on its wrap parameters.
