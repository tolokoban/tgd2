# [TgdPainterMeshes](docs/classes/TgdPainterMeshes.html)

<Demo />

Paint a list of meshes, each one with its own geometry, material and transfo.
Meshes sharing the same material share the same program.

A mesh can have `children`: they are painted after their parent and their `transfo` is relative to the parent's one.
If `visible` is false, the mesh and all its children are hidden.
A mesh without `material` uses the one of its closest ancestor.
A mesh without `geometry` is not painted, but its children are. Use it to group meshes.

Here, a box with a `TgdMaterialNormals` orbits around a pulsing Suzanne with a `TgdMaterialFlatTexture`.
The sphere is a child of the big one, so it follows it and uses its material.
