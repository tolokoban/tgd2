# [TgdFilterDeferredShading](docs/classes/TgdFilterDeferredShading.html)

Physically based lighting computed in screen space from the textures of a
[TgdPainterDeferredShadingTextures](#/articles/painter/deferred-shading).
The main texture of the `TgdPainterFilter` is `textureAlbedo`, and the filter reads the normals, the depth,
the metallic, the roughness, the specular and the baked ambient occlusion from the other textures.

<Demo />

The light comes from two sources:

- `light`: a directional `TgdLight`. Its `direction` is in world space, and the alpha of its `color` is the intensity.
- `skyboxTexture`: a `TgdTextureCube` used for the reflections on metallic surfaces (blurrier with a higher roughness)
  and for the global illumination. If you give a color instead, a cube texture of 1×1 pixels is created with this color.
  `skyboxTransfo` orients the skybox.
  The light coming from the skybox is `skyboxStrength * pow(skyboxTexture.rgb, skyboxExponent)`
  (defaults: `skyboxStrength = 1` and `skyboxExponent = 2.2`).

The specular parameter sets the reflectance of non metallic surfaces: 0.5 gives the usual 4%.

Where nothing has been painted, the filter outputs a transparent black. The demo uses an alpha blending
to see the skybox behind the meshes.

On the box, metallic grows from left to right, and roughness from top to bottom.
