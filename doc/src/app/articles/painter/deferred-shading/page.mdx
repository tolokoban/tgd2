# [TgdPainterDeferredShading](docs/classes/TgdPainterDeferredShading.html)

Framebuffer that holds the surface normals and the depth of meshes, seen from the camera of the context.
It is the first pass of a deferred shading pipeline.

<Demo />

- `textureNormals` (`RGB10_A2`) holds the normals in the camera space, encoded as `N * 0.5 + 0.5`.
  The alpha channel is 1 where a mesh has been painted, and 0 elsewhere.
- `textureDepth` (`R32F`) holds the distance from the camera along its -Z axis,
  normalized between 0 (near plane) and 1 (far plane).

Here, the left half of the screen shows the normals and the right half shows the depth.
